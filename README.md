A pitch for Uber-for-houseplants: $9.99 a month to dispatch a stranger to your apartment to whisper encouragement to your fern, rate its aura, and leave a gold star on the pot. The founder-certain punchline is that the monstera needed a life coach, not water — a fully staffed, fully priced service solving a problem no plant has ever had.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the single app pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
